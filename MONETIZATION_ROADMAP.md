### Tool 2: The Real Estate Factory (Target: Boutique Brokerages)

- **Execution:** Offer a "Bulk Inventory Audit." Have them send a CSV of 10 "stale" listings. 
- **Closing:** Run them through the "Luxury Minimalist" voice. Show them the "Before vs. After." Sell the $99/mo "Agency Tier" for the whole team.

### Tool 3: The Email Factory (Target: High-Ticket Freelancers)
