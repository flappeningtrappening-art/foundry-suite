# FINAL PRODUCTION DESCRIPTIONS (POD RUN #1)

## 1. THE OBSIDIAN SCRIBE
Its chassis, a monolithic sculpture of volcanic glass, absorbed the city's sick luminescence, only to re-emit it in fractured, oily sheens of deep violet and bruised midnight. Each facet, honed to a razor's edge, caught the distorted glare of rain-streaked holograms. 

## 2. THE SILICON SYBIL
A spectral, humanoid presence, its chassis not merely translucent, but a lattice of interwoven fiber-optic strands, pulsing with an internal, cerulean luminescence. Around it float myriad data shards – iridescent, fragmented polygons of light.

## 3. THE CALICHE WARDEN
Heavy-gauge iron plates encrusted with thick, ochre-red rust that bleeds into deeper tones of verdigris. Its sensors are two large, elliptical apertures that glow with an intense, molten amber luminescence.

## 4. SHOP BANNER: NEON MESA ABATTOIR-OASIS
A colossal, tiered construct of pitted chrome and dark ceramite. Cascades of raw, electric cyan and arterial magenta light spill from within its impossible geometries onto plazas of polished black obsidian.
